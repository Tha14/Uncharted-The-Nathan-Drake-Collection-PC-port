**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer

- **Faster import, especially on hard drives** (thanks to Tha14, #9). The level and actor steps read their source
  files into memory once instead of several times, and write their results one after another. On our test PC the
  largest level step went from about 5 minutes to about 2.5; the result is byte for byte the same.
- **Black or flat shading fixed on converted props and vehicles** (thanks to 33martyr, #10): the gatling turret,
  T-62 tank, Mi-24, Village buildings, destructibles, police hats, pirate armour and others now get their proper
  normals and blend layer. The update rebuilds those actor files; the rest of your import is kept.

## Launcher

- **Launcher updates no longer use Python at all.** Installing an update is done by the launcher's own installer
  (`tools\apply_update.exe`), also for older launchers updating to this version, and old shortcuts still open the
  launcher.
