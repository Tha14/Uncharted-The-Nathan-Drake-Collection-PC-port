**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer

- **Multiplayer build v7.179**: the next large part of the multiplayer helper was reimplemented from scratch in C++: the friend list, presence, players met and the friend header; kill rewards, boosters, weapon-category medals, points and awards; the LAN room client and the LAN backend; the connection monitor and net time sync; Play Online and the sign-in wait; the lobby scene and the lobby character re-dress; territory colours; emblems on characters and the emblem manager; rank badges, hat-stat icons, tab boxes, progress rings and the spotted icon; the co-op hunter class list and spectate; the Cinema film list and cameras; mission messages; and the player-state sync. They behave exactly as before; if something in these areas looks different, please send your `boot.log`.
