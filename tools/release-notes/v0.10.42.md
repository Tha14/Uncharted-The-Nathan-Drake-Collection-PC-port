**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer (build v7.97)

- **The download no longer contains any values from the game.** A few values the game uses for its online profiles now come from your own PS3 game files on your PC, when your multiplayer image is built, and never leave it. Nothing changes in game.
- **Nothing to do when you update.** The launcher takes those values from your current multiplayer image, so the update works even if you no longer have your decrypted EBOOT. New setups take them from the EBOOT chosen in step 2, as before.
- After the update, the launcher relinks the multiplayer game by itself, which takes a few seconds.
