**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer

- **Multiplayer build v7.139**: many more parts of the multiplayer helper were reimplemented from scratch in C++: the friend list and its order, the statistics card, the co-op difficulty vote, the Cinema film list and dates, the server list, LAN addresses, session-server messages, kill rewards and weapon statistics, the lobby menus, taunts, rank badges, invites, prompts, hat stats, the objective ring, matchmaking start, the replay clock and more. They behave exactly as before; if something in these areas looks different, please send your `boot.log`.
