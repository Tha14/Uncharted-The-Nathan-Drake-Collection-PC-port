**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 co-op (multiplayer build v7.111)

- **Knocked-down teammate**: a downed teammate's ring now shows the knocked-down icon, as on PS3 (the PS4 code had no icon there). PS3's triangle "revive" icon when you are close enough is not in yet.
- **Checkpoint ring colour**: another fix for the white checkpoint ring in Co-op Adventure. It should now take its red colour; if it stays white, please send your `boot.log` (the "U3MP r01" lines show why).
- Your multiplayer files are updated automatically after the launcher update (a quick relink, no full import).
