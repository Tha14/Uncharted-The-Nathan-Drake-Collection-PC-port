**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Launcher and games

- **FSR 4 removed**: the FSR 4 and FSR 4.1.1 upscalers are no longer included (the license of their model files is disputed). If you had FSR 4 selected, the game switches to FSR 3.1 by itself; DLSS / DLAA, FSR 3.1 and TAA are unchanged. Gameplay is not affected.
- **Uncharted 2 online** now runs as a separate program (`u2_online.exe`) next to the game instead of inside it. Nothing changes in how you play; if Uncharted 2 online stops working for you, please send your `boot.log`.
- The launcher's Credits now say clearly that this is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment, Naughty Dog or Bluepoint Games.
