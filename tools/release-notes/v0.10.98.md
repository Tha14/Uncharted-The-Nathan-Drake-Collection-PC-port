**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer

- **Multiplayer build v7.123**: more parts of the multiplayer helper were reimplemented from scratch in C++ (loadout click sounds, booster icons and level discs, the weapon statistics page, death animations, Cinema film buttons, the invite list's time text, the friend list order, the EULA pages, the profile box XP bar, the map vote, matchmaking rows and more). They behave exactly as before; if something in these areas looks different, please send your `boot.log`.
- **Fixed**: two internal copies (friend list, received messages) could be reordered by the compiler; they are now safe.
- **Online**: if the file with your own session value (`u3-session.key`, made by the launcher from your PS3 EBOOT) is missing, the RPCN sign-in now says so in `boot.log` instead of failing without a reason.
