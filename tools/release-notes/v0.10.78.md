**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Launcher tabs regrouped

- **Graphics**: Display and Performance in one tab, with texture filtering, depth of field and cutscene stretch. Settings fewer players change fold away under "More frame rate settings" and "Counter style".
- **Game**: language, save profile, Uncharted 3 exposure, speakers, the in-game menu shortcut and Uncharted 2's torch.
- **Experimental** (new, before Multiplayer): features still being tested (two render threads, lower video memory, lamp shadows, full-resolution textures). A feature moves to its usual tab once it is reliable.
- **Setup**: finished steps get a check mark and the next one says "start here"; the launcher opens on Setup until your game folder and update 1.02 are in place.
- **Multiplayer**: a checklist at the top shows what is set up and what is missing.
- **Launcher**: now holds the performance log and the credits. Your settings are unchanged.
