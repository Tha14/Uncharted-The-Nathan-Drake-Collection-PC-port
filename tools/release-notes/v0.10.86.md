**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Joining matches and a lobby crash

- **Joining a friend's match in progress**: you no longer get "You were removed from the game" after the countdown. The host now brings you into the running match once your game has loaded.
- **Following the host into a match**: players who joined the party are no longer dropped while the match loads. Their game now waits until it is connected to the host before loading, as on PS3.
- **Lobby crash**: fixed a crash that could happen in the multiplayer lobby ("read access to 0x18 at guest offset 0xe8fc12").
- If joining still fails, both players' `boot.log` files now say exactly who left or removed whom; please send both.
