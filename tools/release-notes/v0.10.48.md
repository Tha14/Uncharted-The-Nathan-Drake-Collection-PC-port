**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Controls

- **Every controller button can be rebound now, L2, R2 and the sticks too.** On the Controls page, click a slot and press a button or trigger. For Move and Look, move a stick: Move and Look each take a whole stick, so you can swap them. A trigger can do any action and any button can aim or fire. The in-game menu keeps these choices.

## Launcher

- **Controller detection on native code:** the Controls page finds your controller and its buttons through the launcher's own native code.
