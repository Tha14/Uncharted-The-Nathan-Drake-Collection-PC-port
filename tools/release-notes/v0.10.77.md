**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Launcher layout: Setup tab, Cheats, Controls

- **Setup tab** (was Quick Setup): the first steps in one place: 1. your game folder (or install it from its .pkg, check its files), 2. update 1.02, 3. a quality level. Tools keeps shortcuts, Steam and troubleshooting (now with the log and save folders).
- **Cheats**: Unlock all chapters / rewards moved here from Advanced, next to the developer switches.
- **Controls**: the bindings span the window, movement and weapons on the left, actions on the right, lined up as a grid.
- **No more notch** at the top and bottom edge of full-width cards.
- **Multiplayer**: step 1 explains which two folders to add and what each holds.
- **US copies**: the CUSA02343 (US) 1.02 game executables are identical to CUSA02320's, so US copies work for multiplayer too.
- **Crash reports**: when the game ends through a C++ terminate, the log now says where it came from.
