**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Uncharted 3 multiplayer

- **Multiplayer build v7.151**: the rest of this batch of the multiplayer helper was reimplemented from scratch in C++: saving your multiplayer profile, the online profile download, the server sign-in and session hello, the matchmaking players list, the menu actions and the clan tag keyboard, party invites and joins (including held join requests), the Network Profile and Records pages, the player card tabs, the whole Cinema flow (film list, select, protect, delete, recording start), the spotted icon on the HUD, wall emblems on the maps, the slider track on the emblem page, the death animation check, the LAN sockets and the anim-state sync entry points. They behave exactly as before; if something in these areas looks different, please send your `boot.log`.
