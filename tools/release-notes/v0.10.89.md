**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## Connecting to other players

- **Connection between players**: the game now keeps trying to connect to the players in your party or match instead of giving up after a few attempts, and tries again when someone joins later.
- **Router port**: when a connection gets no answer, the game asks your router (UPnP) to open its multiplayer port (UDP 3658) by itself. This helps players whose routers blocked each other. If your router has UPnP off, forwarding UDP 3658 to your PC by hand does the same.
- **Starting a match together**: a player whose connection isn't up yet gets 20 seconds before being removed, instead of being removed at once.
- **When no connection can be made**: you see "Unable to join party" and leave the party, or return to the lobby from a match start, instead of being left half-joined.
- These apply to party joins, Custom Game, Find Game and joining a match in progress.
- If two players still can't connect, please send both `boot.log` files and `analysis/uncharted-user/data/u3-peer-link.txt` from both PCs.
