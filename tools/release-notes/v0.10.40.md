**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer maps (overlay recipe 1.13.0)

- **The sun-blocker fix from London Underground now covers every map that needs it** (thanks to 33martyr): 20 map files in all, including Airstrip, Highrise, Yemen and several co-op maps. Invisible shadow casters now block the sun again, so it no longer floods indoor areas. After the update, the launcher reapplies the multiplayer import by itself.
- Some of these maps may still look off in places. That is known and being looked at.

## Launcher

- **Run only one launcher at a time.** Two launchers each sign in to RPCN with your account, and the game then shows "No connection found".
- The Uncharted 3 Multiplayer tab no longer loads the online module a second time, which could leave the game without the launcher's RPCN session.
