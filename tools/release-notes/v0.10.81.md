**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** unofficial PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.
## RPCN accounts and servers, co-op and after-match fixes

- **RPCN online play section** (both games): choose the RPCN server like in RPCS3 (Official or your own, add / delete, or import RPCS3's list), save several accounts, pick a default and switch between them. The layout is tidier: status first, then server, account, account creation (folded), friends. Your saved login carries over.
- **Co-op**: while a grabber holds you, the blue ring shows the fist icon again, as on PS3.
- **After-match report**: the prestige badge's gold background shows behind your level again.
